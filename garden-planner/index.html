About this free garden planner

The Croaking Acres Garden Planner is a free planning tool for home gardeners and small market growers. Draw your garden to scale, decide what goes where, and turn it into a season of dated tasks. It runs entirely in your browser with no account, and your plan never leaves your device.

Lay out your space

Add beds, paths, hoop houses and irregular growing areas on a one-foot grid. Set a north arrow, lock shapes in place, and see square footage and how much of each area is beds.

Plan every planting

Pick from about 50 common crops or add your own. Plants are drawn at true spacing with row and plant counts, and you can split a bed, stagger rows or repeat a crop across beds for succession planting.

A calendar from your frost dates

Seed-starting, transplant and harvest dates come from your last spring and first fall frost. Beds in a hoop house get a longer season, and the planner suggests whether to transplant or direct sow.

Tasks, records and a forecast

Check off tasks by day, week or bed, schedule care jobs like irrigation and cover crops, export a calendar file or printable plan, keep a field log, and project harvest sales.

Common questions

Is the garden planner free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no sign-up and no ads.

Where is my garden plan saved?

In your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Save plan file or Copy plan to keep a backup or move your plan to another device.

Does it work on a phone or offline?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, can be installed to a phone's home screen, and keeps working offline once it has loaded.

How does it decide when to plant?

Enter your last spring and first fall frost dates. Each crop has typical timing, such as weeks before or after the last frost and days to harvest, and the planner sets seed-starting, planting and harvest dates from those. Beds inside a hoop house get a longer season. You can change any date or number.

Can I put the planting schedule in my calendar?

Yes. Download a calendar file (.ics) with one event per task and import it into Google, Apple or Outlook calendar, or print a weekly or monthly plan.

Can I add a crop that is not in the list?

Yes. Add your own crops with your spacing, frost tolerance, days to harvest, harvest window and map color, and they appear in every crop menu.

Calendar file (.ics)

One all-day event per action. Each lists the beds and rows, and harvest is one event spanning its window. Import it into Google, Apple or Outlook calendar, or whichever calendar your Skylight shows. If saving renames it to .txt, change the ending back to .ics. Each event keeps the same ID, so importing again later updates it.
