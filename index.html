Garden Planner

Lay out your beds, hoop houses and paths to scale, then plan every planting through the season.

  • Plants drawn at true spacing, with row and plant counts
  • Season calendar built from your own frost dates
  • Task list, calendar file and a printable weekly plan
  • Field Log for notes and a harvest sales forecast
